“Oceans Rise, Empires Fall”

January 27, 2019

Darkness fell over the earth. The floor beneath split in perfect unison with the skies. A cloth somewhere tore. Knees fell to the ground. “Surely these were a righteous people.” I was shaken but oddly indifferent also. I saw the world as one would in a moving bus with its aisle lights on: the truth of what I was feeling overshadowing the world as it really was; without a careful focus on the reflection of the inside of the bus, or on the world outside, all one sees is a superposition of both – which is the truth of neither reality.

Through the collapse of the empire I lived through memories I was aware would hold power over me, even as they were being made, long after the people in them drifted away. The clock struck three. The last of the empire went up in flames. I wasn’t sure if it was okay for me to stand up then, so I continued to kneel. The sun would come out soon, and then I would know. The smoke was so thick, and the fires so wild that not too much ahead was visible.

The foundations of the empire had been my foundation for so long.

I tried to remember the succession of events as they had been before the collapse. The only thing that came to mind was a certain cock’s crow. I decided instead to focus on life as it might be with a collapsed empire. Was it survival instincts that insisted I run? I didn’t acknowledge the shame that accompanied that thought. There wasn’t much sense in moaning its death anyway.

The sun came out just in time to shed light on the empire breathing its last. A sudden downpour of rain washed away the already starving flames. The view from outside the compounds was chilling in its unfamiliarity.

I made a silent wish with regard to the spirits of the empire and the heavens above. And then I stood up, dusted my knees, and walked away.
