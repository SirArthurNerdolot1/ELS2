The Ticket, the Jacket, and the Can

May 27, 2020

Chaos, it is what God wants or maybe we do or perhaps believe that He wants. Who are we to say anyway? He created us in his image or did We? Alright this is a…a…..a detour, come back, there is a story about to occur and you do not want to be a part of any philosophical derailing, I mean discourse. Remember, this is not happening just because you are reading this. This is the second coming of Valmiki but the author has died, as Barthes wanted.

It was a great day, a red sky, making the pale french girls look like they were blushing, maybe they were. A hot day with an imminent chilly wind, a messenger of the winter, riffed a few beats through the fiery locks of my old love. We were on the way back from that over-aged film, “The Butterfly Effect”, our first one together back in the golden days, as they are for everyone. Looking back at it, it was a shitty pact to rewatch it after more than a decade. So much that we just crushed the tickets like we wanted to…ummm…. and tossed it away into the woods. It seemed to resemble the stale and wrinkling bond we now had, as the Man already said, “Bare laid the ruins, where late the sweet birds sang”. He said a lot, correction, wrote a lot and still managed to be true, truer than the phoenix’s tears which couldn’t heal the ravaged heart which somehow managed to beat inside. This storm of thoughts went on and I stopped for a drink, nothing better than an icy coke and rum to cool off and heat up. Got the singular red and a cask of rum to go, a can too much. The drink worked its miracle, although the signature red can wasn’t dry and it just had to get on my jacket, thanks to the stupid bitch who always finds a way to fuck it all up.

As I said, it was getting cold, a jacket became a necessity and I just Kobe’ed the can into the trashcan before trotting off into the nearest store I could see which just had to be the very same one where I bought this ruined jacket with this witch. You get me, right? I am sure you do. I bought the exact same one so it goes along well with the rest. I could be lucky because it was the last piece they had, nothing more would ever be made. Sometime later, someone else, somebody higher up, walked into the store to get a jacket. Well, you know what’s next, great men think alike and consequently, wear alike but it was out of the bounds of heavens for him.

The next day he went cold to his fancy office, worked up and trying to warm his hands by blowing into his interlocked fists. A sort out day’s work ahead piled in front, he started to work his way through it. The clock went round, time off and the mechanism’s works. It is a regular day by all means, nothing much apart from the seldom tense air which was glowing that day. A mistake had been made, nothing much. just a misplaced punctuation, That’s all. Obviously, it was the cold man. There’s more to it, the file was to be sent to the Queen. Yes, this is where it blows up. The can finds its way to contribute, to find its meaning. Aluminium can be a catalyst to a great many organic reactions, as if it wants to. It finds its way to the land of cheap labour, sorry, world’s manufacturing backbone to be recycled and given a new purpose. It works its wonders

instead. Brings about a chemical reaction which alters the DNA in just the right way to end a third of the population the world across.

Fun fact: tickets are usually printed on thermal paper as opposed to those hard stubs there were in the Golden Age of Hollywood. It’s in the details, you will see. The tickets flew in the breeze until it all curled up into a heap with dry leaves. Now, the thing with dry leaves of the species found in the area is they burst up and thermal paper catches sparks quite readily. Hot day, dry leaves, thermal paper, is this a conspiracy or what? Fire broke out in the area and is nature’s doing, nothing more. Well, humans are nature and vice versa, after all. It has been months since the fire now, hopefully it will soon go off due to the blast. Wait, it’s not a blast, it’s the blow of the horn at dusk. Humanity’s dusk.

This is, my friend, the miracle of language. A single punctuation or grammatical error and English go haywire. Now, what does the heavy crown do when it lazily rests on a head all its life? It blocks the flow of blood to the brain. Now, this is a nice thing for someone to write on. Behold, a monarch with hindered cognitive abilities, meets a fun orangutan and they have tea in the middle of one of the most detailed to be awfully boring house. And she bobs her head sleepily into the tea. The documents to be honoured with their signatures are brought in and they mindlessly do it as they are. The errors are exploited and the video games are played.

When exactly are you, you might be wondering. Now is where you are, a wild-wild wildfire, a big red button begging to be pushed and an unstoppable virus just out there. So, who is responsible, Lorenz? Satan? It is you. How else would I know what the cold man did, where the breeze took the tickets, why are societies hierarchical.
