THE KING OF PANDEMONIUM

October 30, 2018

Have you ever ventured into roads, Dark and obscure , My fiend heart reigns in them, Malevolent and ugly possessions galore , My pernicious heart trades in them . I stand honored , Setting sadistic paradigm, My virtue is sin, sinister I am. More schadenfreude sentiments slip in . I tempered with the decorum , He showed me his prowess, All I was in the midst of, was my creative process, And I was thrown in dungeon In his momentary triumph, He did foster , in his very holster, The rise of pandemonium. Creative process you beseech? Ever fancied spearheading anarchy? Its zenith hoisted on the tip of….. Gucci! Confession: Needed something to rhyme with, Even the Devil wears Prada you see… So off I went into rebellion , and distressed thronged in the pandemonium , I don’t reminisce realms of happier times, for the simple reason, I’ve never quite had them, Or it might be a queer case of dementia , memory intact , devoid of euphoria…. So I did venture into protest, but alas, luck wasn’t my side that day, my curt father banished me to desert , Which now is beaming with Agonizing frenzy by the way.. And then, Hold your breath , sit and behold , * drum rolls* an interesting anecdote is to unfold, the insinuation bestowed on me , left me dried and parched , because my blood had boiled away in despair, There’s no one to care…. I often mulled over, as the ruler of my clan , what’s it is like to have a concerned glance over the usual scared stares, theres no one who cares…. Your onus might weigh a ton , but standing against your parent is gargantuan , Even for me, also for the Satan., It is no mean feat to rule the pandemonium, By Hades, I mustn’t let loose my fault lines , what if someone taps into them! Ironic no? I would’ve done the same . After all I am the king of pandemonium, a fact of glory albeit quite lame. Though there are times , I do wonder what’s is in the name , what’s in the name?? A story to tell, a secret to hide , I preside those corners where once you’ve cried , Abhor me , I welcome your loathe , Admit , we have all that darker side, gluttony, lust or sheer spite, So why obscure the view , from the blue hue of you? Welcome henceforth, you haven’t such options often, When we’ve got a hell to reign, Why serve in heaven??
