Poetry Week 2025 – A Celebration of Verse and Voice

Poetry Week was a week-long celebration of poetic expression that brought together voices from within and beyond the campus community. Held online, the event featured a series of creative prompts released every two days on our Instagram handle, encouraging writers to respond with original poetry.

The response was overwhelming, submissions poured in from students and poetry enthusiasts across the country. Each entry reflected a unique blend of imagination, emotion, and craft, making Poetry Week not just a showcase of talent but a space for literary connection and creative exploration.

Through this initiative, ELS aimed to foster a culture of poetic expression and provide a platform for emerging voices. We thank all the participants for making it a truly memorable event and look forward to hosting more such initiatives in the future.
