Chronicle · March 18, 2025

Scrabble League'25

IITK Scrabble League 2025 marked a delightful confluence of strategy, vocabulary, and community spirit. Designed to welcome newcomers while challenging seasoned players, the tournament served as an accessible yet competitive entry point into the world of Scrabble. The event was especially notable for the number of first-time participants. Many joined with little more than love. Despite the competitive nature of the tournament, the atmosphere remained encouraging and inclusive. From closely contested matches to surprising upsets, the league balanced learning with fun, fostering both skill development and camaraderie.
