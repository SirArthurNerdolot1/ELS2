Kavita Kisse Kahaniyan

ELS took nine voices to Lucknow for Kavita Kisse Kahaniyan. One stage, a trail of pieces: Aashvi’s Map of Home, Kavya Yadav’s Born for the Pyre, Ishita Singh’s Pure, Aashi Sikriwal’s Unfit in the Crowd, Mudita Roy’s Twenty, Kushagra Chandra’s Punchlines, Sahishnu Chaturvedula’s Kaleidoscope, Soumil Aggarwal’s Will the Haze End?, and Kanishk Sharma’s The Tick.

Lucknow was, they said afterwards, an absolute dream. Until the next mic drops.
