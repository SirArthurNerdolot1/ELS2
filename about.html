About Us

The English Literary Society at IIT Kanpur was founded with the mission to celebrate literature and foster a community of literary enthusiasts. We organize workshops, poetry readings, creative writing sessions, and much more to engage students and faculty alike.

Our society welcomes members from all backgrounds and skill levels. Whether you are a seasoned writer or just discovering your love for literature, you’ll find a supportive community here.

We believe in the power of stories to connect, inspire, and transform individuals and societies. Join us to share your voice and discover new perspectives.
