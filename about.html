About Us

The English Literary Society at IIT Kanpur was founded to celebrate literature and foster a vibrant community of readers and writers.

We organize workshops, poetry readings, creative writing sessions, and more for students and faculty alike.

Whether you're a seasoned writer or a curious beginner, ELS welcomes you to explore, express, and engage with the literary world.
