Dandelion

Dandelion is the annual literary magazine of the English Literary Society at IIT Kanpur. It captures the introspections, imaginations, and inquiries of the campus community — a space where creative minds express themselves through poetry, prose, essays, and visual art. Each issue is a delicate bloom of thoughts, crafted with care, intellect, and emotion, fluttering like seeds in the wind — resonating with every reader in a different way.
